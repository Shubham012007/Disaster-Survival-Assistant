🌊 Flood Safety Guide

  • Move to a safe and higher location.
  • Avoid walking or driving through flood water.
  • Switch off electricity if safe to do so.
  • Keep drinking water and emergency supplies ready.
  • Follow official emergency instructions.

🌍 Earthquake Safety Guide

  • Drop, Cover and Hold On.
  • Stay away from windows and glass.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • After shaking stops, move carefully to a safe area.
  • Follow official emergency instructions.

🔥 Fire Safety Guide

  • Leave the building if there is danger.
  • Avoid breathing smoke.
  • Stay low if there is smoke.
  • Do not use elevators during a fire.
  • Contact emergency services.

🌀 Cyclone Safety Guide

  • Stay indoors and away from windows.
  • Follow official weather and emergency alerts.
  • Keep emergency food and water ready.
  • Charge your phone and keep a power bank ready.
  • Do not go outside until authorities say it is safe.
